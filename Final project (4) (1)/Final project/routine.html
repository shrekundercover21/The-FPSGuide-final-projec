    Daily Mechanical Training

    Consistency comes from repetition. Follow this 15-minute daily routine before entering ranked play.

    1. 5 Minutes: Static clicking to warm up wrist precision.
    2. 5 Minutes: Micro-flick drills to improve reaction time.
    3. 5 Minutes: Tracking moving targets at varied distances.
